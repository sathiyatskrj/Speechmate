# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our Pledge

We as members, contributors, and leaders pledge to make participation in our community a harassment-free experience for everyone, regardless of age, body size, visible or invisible disability, ethnicity, sex characteristics, gender identity and expression, level of experience, education, socio-economic status, nationality, personal appearance, race, caste, color, religion, or sexual identity and orientation.

We pledge to act and interact in ways that contribute to an open, welcoming, diverse, inclusive, and healthy community.

## Our Standards

Examples of behavior that contributes to a positive environment:

* Using welcoming and inclusive language
* Being respectful of differing viewpoints and experiences
* Gracefully accepting constructive criticism
* Focusing on what is best for the community and for indigenous language preservation
* Showing empathy towards other community members
* Respecting indigenous cultural protocols and data sovereignty

Examples of unacceptable behavior:

* The use of sexualized language or imagery, and sexual attention or advances of any kind
* Trolling, insulting or derogatory comments, and personal or political attacks
* Public or private harassment
* Publishing others' private information without explicit permission
* Misuse, commercial exploitation, or AI training on indigenous linguistic data without proper consent (see [DATA_TERMS.txt](DATA_TERMS.txt))
* Other conduct which could reasonably be considered inappropriate in a professional setting

## Indigenous Data Respect

SpeechMate contains linguistic data from **endangered Nicobarese and Great Andamanese languages**. Contributors must:

* Respect the **CC BY-NC 4.0 + Traditional Knowledge** licensing on all linguistic data
* Not use dictionary entries, audio recordings, or cultural content for commercial purposes or AI/LLM training
* Acknowledge and respect the cultural significance of the languages being preserved
* Follow any tribal council protocols when contributing new linguistic data

## Enforcement Responsibilities

Community leaders are responsible for clarifying and enforcing our standards of acceptable behavior and will take appropriate and fair corrective action in response to any behavior that they deem inappropriate, threatening, offensive, or harmful.

## Scope

This Code of Conduct applies within all community spaces, and also applies when an individual is officially representing the community in public spaces.

## Enforcement

Instances of abusive, harassing, or otherwise unacceptable behavior may be reported to the project maintainers via [GitHub Issues](https://github.com/sathiyatskrj/Speechmate/issues) or by contacting the maintainer directly.

All complaints will be reviewed and investigated promptly and fairly. All community leaders are obligated to respect the privacy and security of the reporter of any incident.
